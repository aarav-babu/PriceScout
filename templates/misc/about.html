About

Pricing second-hand things,
without the guesswork

PriceScout combines live market data with machine learning to estimate a fair resale value for cars, phones and laptops — locally and nationally, in seconds.

Research & origin

Backed by published research

PriceScout started as my capstone project, and the approach behind it was published as a peer-reviewed IEEE paper. I'm now rebuilding it from the ground up with a much better experience — cleaner design, a faster flow, and a hosting-ready foundation.
