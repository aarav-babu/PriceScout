About

Pricing second-hand things,
without the guesswork

PriceScout began with a capstone question: how can resale research stay useful when market prices keep changing? The public app now compares current car asking prices and saves phone and laptop details.

Research & origin

Backed by published research

I’m Aarav Babu, the sole maintainer of PriceScout today. The idea grew out of a capstone project on web data and machine learning for resale pricing, documented in the linked IEEE paper. I’m continuing that research as a publicly hosted application.

01

Real market data

Current listing data helps reveal changes that an older dataset can miss. Listings show seller asking prices, not completed sales.

02

Machine learning

The research explored regression on query-specific used-car data. The current public release shows observed prices; ML valuations remain separate.

03

Trusted & transparent

Source links, collection timestamps, and explicit unavailable states help you judge what the available evidence can support.

The journey

  1. Capstone project

  2. Published in IEEE

    The research was published at IEEE AIIoT in 2024, with small-dataset limitations documented in the paper.
