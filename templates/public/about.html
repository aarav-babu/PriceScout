OUR STORY

From a capstone project
to a useful everyday tool.

PriceScout began as a capstone project exploring web data and machine learning for resale pricing. Today, we’re building a simpler way to research used products and keep their details organized.

The current release lets you compare actual car asking prices in India and save phones and laptops in a private collection.
